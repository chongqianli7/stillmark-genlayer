# Stillmark

Category: Identity & Reputation / Other

A shared condition-handover desk for loaned objects. Two wallets independently sign outgoing and incoming reports. GenLayer agrees on the tagged item's identity and each inspected part's condition; deterministic rules retain new differences, block uncertainty and require both parties to acknowledge the same comparison digest.

The interface includes an offline tutorial, wallet and temporary test sessions, finalized public-record lookup, source-verified contract calls, persistent transaction tracking and JSON export. The demonstration uses authored reports about a tagged lamp, not a real physical inspection.

Application: https://chongqianli7.github.io/stillmark-genlayer/

Repository: https://github.com/chongqianli7/stillmark-genlayer
